These pages explain the terms and relationships you need for SPL Sense and SPL Cloud. They do not replace specialist
literature. In Switzerland, the V-NISSG ordinance and the enforcement guide of the Federal Office of Public Health
(BAG/FOPH) apply; in Germany, DIN 15905-5. Which templates SPL Sense provides for them is described under
[Templates for V-NISSG and DIN 15905-5]({{< relref "templates-vnissg-din" >}}).
