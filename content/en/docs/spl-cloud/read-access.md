With read access, another person sees a measurement or a whole job without being able to change anything. Typical for
organizers, operators or authorities.

There are two kinds:

| | Read access | Shared link |
|---|---|---|
| For | people with an SPL Cloud account | anyone who has the link or QR code |
| Cost | free | [SPL Cloud Pro]({{< relref "/docs/spl-cloud-pro/shared-links" >}}) |
| Revoking | per person | per link, or with an expiration date |

This page describes the free read access.

## Granting read access

1. Open the measurement or the job.
2. Menu **Measurement** or **Job** › **Manage Read Access**.
3. **Add Read Access**.
4. Search for the person by email address. They must have an SPL Cloud account; if not, ask them to create one.
5. **Select scope**: only this measurement or the whole job. Access to the job also applies to measurements added later.
6. **Save**.

The **Read Access Users** list shows who has access, since when and who granted it. **Remove** revokes it.

Anyone who may manage the measurement can grant read access: the owner, or all members of the organization that owns
it.

## What readers see

People with read access find the measurement on their overview. They see all data, days, events and pictures and can
download CSV exports, and with Pro also day reports. They cannot change anything, pass on access, or complete or delete
the measurement.
