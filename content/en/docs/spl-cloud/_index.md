SPL Cloud at [spl.cloud](https://spl.cloud) archives the measurements from SPL Sense and makes them available in the
browser, from anywhere and for everyone you give access to. It runs in parallel to SPL Sense: measuring always happens
locally, and the cloud receives the data continuously as soon as internet is available.

Everything in this chapter is free, except where Pro is noted. Additional features such as day reports, alarms, remote
control or shareable links are available per measurement with [SPL Cloud Pro]({{< relref "/docs/spl-cloud-pro" >}}).

This chapter is aimed at professionals. To get started, the [quick start]({{< relref "/docs/quick-start" >}}) is enough.
