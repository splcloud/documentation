## Day reports

In SPL Sense, the day report as PDF is free. In SPL Cloud it is available with Pro, in two variants:

- **Day report**: **PDF** button at the selected day. Without Pro, the button is greyed out.
- **Day report with deviations**: menu **Measurement** › **Open day report with deviations**. Additionally contains the
  exceedances of the value profile thresholds for the selected day.

If the measurement belongs to an organization with a logo, the logo appears in the report. You set the logo on the
[organization]({{< relref "/docs/spl-cloud/organizations#logo" >}}) page.

## Pictures for the measurement

Pictures document how the measurement was made: the measuring location, the sensor, the sound system, the record of the
calibration run.

1. Open the measurement. On the side is the **Pictures** area.
2. Drag photos into the area or click **+**.
3. Clicking a picture opens it large. There you add a **Caption** and **Save**.

| | |
|---|---|
| Formats | JPEG and PNG. Share or export HEIC photos from an iPhone as JPEG first. |
| Size | at most 20 MB per picture |
| Number | at most 100 pictures per measurement |

Anyone who sees the measurement sees the pictures, also via read access and shared links. Only people who may manage
the measurement can add pictures, and only with Pro. Deleting (**Delete picture?**) removes the picture permanently.

## Pictures report

Menu **Measurement** › **Open pictures report** creates a PDF with all pictures of the measurement and their captions.
Together with the day report, this gives complete documentation of the measurement.
