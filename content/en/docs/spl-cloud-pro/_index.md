The basic SPL Cloud is free. With **Pro** you extend individual measurements with additional features, without a
subscription and without a contract. Billing is per measurement and measuring day.

| Feature | In short |
|---|---|
| [Shared links]({{< relref "shared-links" >}}) | Access via link or QR code, without an account |
| [Reports and pictures]({{< relref "reports-and-pictures" >}}) | Day reports as PDF, also with deviations; pictures for the measurement and pictures report |
| [Alarms]({{< relref "alarms" >}}) | Email and SMS on exceedance or failure |
| [Remote control]({{< relref "remote-control" >}}) | Control SPL Sense from the cloud, comments and excluded time ranges from the cloud |

What Pro costs and how it is billed is described under [Pricing and billing]({{< relref "pricing-and-billing" >}}).
Read that page before activating Pro for the first time.
