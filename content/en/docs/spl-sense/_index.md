SPL Sense is the measurement software for the sensor. It records the sound level, displays it live, rates it against
your value profile and, if required, controls the volume with the leveler. SPL Sense works without internet; with
[SPL Cloud]({{< relref "/docs/spl-cloud" >}}) the data is additionally archived online.

This chapter is aimed at professionals. For your first measurement, the [Quick start]({{< relref "/docs/quick-start" >}})
is enough.
